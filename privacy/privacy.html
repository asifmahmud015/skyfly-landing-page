Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

At SkyFly, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, passport details (for booking).
  • Payment Information: Card details (processed securely via third-party gateways).
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process flight bookings and payments.
  • To send booking confirmations and updates.
  • To improve our website and customer service.
  • To send promotional offers (only with your consent).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Airlines and travel partners to complete your booking.
  • Payment processors for secure transactions.
  • Legal authorities when required by law.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience, remember preferences, and analyze traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We use SSL encryption and industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no online transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

  • Access, update, or delete your personal data.
  • Opt out of marketing emails anytime.
  • Request a copy of your stored data.

7. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.
